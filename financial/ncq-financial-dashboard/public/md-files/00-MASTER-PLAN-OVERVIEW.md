# NCQ Platform - Master Implementation Plan

**Document Version**: 1.0  
**Date**: January 2025  
**Total Duration**: 18 months  
**Total Budget**: $12.5 Million USD

## Executive Summary

The NCQ Platform is a comprehensive SaaS ecosystem designed to drive digital transformation in Saudi Arabia and the broader Middle East region. This master plan outlines the implementation strategy for all components including core technologies, shared services, and individual products.

## Platform Components Overview

### 1. Core Technologies (Foundation Layer)
- **IoT Platform**: Enterprise-grade IoT infrastructure
- **Blockchain**: Corda and Hyperledger implementations
- **Timeline**: 6 months
- **Budget**: $2.5M

### 2. NCQ Platform (Orchestration Layer)
- **Main Platform**: Central management and orchestration
- **Shared Services**: Authentication, Payments, Notifications, Analytics
- **Timeline**: 8 months
- **Budget**: $3M

### 3. Products (Application Layer)
- **NCQ LLM**: AI/ML Platform (4 months, $1.5M)
- **Smart Hospitality**: IoT-enabled hotel/building management (4 months, $1.5M)
- **Hospital Management**: Healthcare system (6 months, $2M)
- **NCQ PGW**: Payment Gateway (3 months, $1M)
- **NCQ Mobile App**: Platform mobile mirror (3 months, $0.5M)
- **NCQ Mobile LLM**: Mobile AI (2 months, $0.5M)
